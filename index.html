01Research Paper — AI Development in the U.S. and China

I participated in a Pioneer program and wrote about this topic. Comparing and contrasting the two nations offers insight into their economic, political, and other social circumstances.

Two Innovation Systems, One AI Rivalry: Comparing Artificial Intelligence Development and Its Economic and Political Consequences in China and the United States

02AI Tutoring Videos and Programs for Senior Netizens

Existing problem: I noticed that my grandparents could not identify AI-generated fake information that seemed to be correct.

Solution: I designed a course incorporating some of senior netizens’ routine needs and teaching them how to verify the authenticity of information.

Outcome: This has helped them avoid online AI fraud and health risks related to incorrect AI responses. The course has been made into online videos and taught in community centers, reaching 150+ elderly residents.

03Researching AI Usage Characteristics Among Senior Netizens

What is it: This is a follow-up project to the previous project. I interviewed and investigated 100+ senior netizens aged 50–70 who attended my course to understand how they use AI tools.

Conclusion:

  1. They use AI for health counseling, random chatting, and everyday life questions rather than studying or making complicated decisions.
  2. They blindly trust AI-generated text, not realizing the limitations of such tools.
  3. They ask overgeneralized questions, failing to formulate detailed and effective prompts.
04Short Video of AI Blind Spot among Senior Netizens

What is it: I used the AI video generation tool Seedance to create two short films. The content includes realistic scenarios of AI scams and misleading health information.

Outcome: Those videos raise awareness of the risks AI can pose to older people’s finances and health, and have received 40,000+ views in total.
